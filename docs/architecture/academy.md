# Hlabi Academy foundation

Hlabi Academy provides a structured learning foundation for people entering property work.

## Current foundation

- Six-module Property Professional Foundation course.
- Authenticated course listing and enrollment API.
- Property-career learning progress events.
- Completion state and refund-eligibility flag in the domain model.
- Public Academy experience with progress interaction.

## Career pathway

Learn → practise → complete → pursue the applicable qualification/registration → gain experience → progress toward agent opportunities.

The Academy foundation does not itself confer a professional designation, registration or qualification. Any PPRA, educational-provider, examination, refund, internship or employment requirements must be implemented and verified separately before launch.

## Refund boundary

`refundEligible` is a product workflow flag only. The previously proposed 50% schooling-fee refund must not be presented as unconditional until commercial terms, qualifying events, payment evidence, exclusions, and applicable consumer/legal requirements are finalized.
