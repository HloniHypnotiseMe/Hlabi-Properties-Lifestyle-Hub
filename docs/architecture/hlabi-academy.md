# Hlabi Academy

Hlabi Academy is the training pathway for people preparing for a property career.

## Foundation
- course catalogue with structured modules
- authenticated enrolment
- module progress tracking
- completion state
- a product-level `refundEligible` marker for future commercial terms

The initial course covers property fundamentals, buyer/seller journeys, listing and marketing practice, negotiation/client communication, ethics/compliance/professional conduct, and AI tools for property work.

## Regulatory and commercial boundaries
The platform does not itself confer a statutory property qualification, licence or registration. Any qualification, examination, internship, refund or employment outcome must be implemented against the applicable provider, regulatory and contractual requirements.

`refundEligible` is only a platform eligibility flag. It is not a payment instruction, guarantee or promise of a refund; actual schooling/refund terms must be separately contracted and verified.

AI assistance should remain reviewable and must not fabricate qualifications, results, regulatory status or employment outcomes.
