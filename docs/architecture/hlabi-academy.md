# Hlabi Academy

Hlabi Academy is the training pathway for people preparing for a property career.

## Foundation
- course catalogue with structured modules
- authenticated enrolment
- module progress tracking
- completion state
- a product-level `refundEligible` marker for future commercial terms

The initial course covers property fundamentals, buyer/seller journeys, listing and marketing practice, negotiation/client communication, ethics/compliance/professional conduct, and AI tools for property work.

## Commercial and regulatory boundaries
The platform does not itself confer a statutory property qualification, registration or licence. Any PPRA-recognised qualification, examination, internship, refund or employment outcome must be implemented against the applicable provider, regulatory and contractual requirements.

`refundEligible` is only a platform eligibility flag. It is not a payment instruction, guarantee or promise of a 50% refund; actual schooling/refund terms must be separately contracted and verified.

## Future layers
- AI-avatar lessons and simulations
- assessments and question banks
- mentor/instructor workflows
- qualification-provider integration
- internship and placement tracking
- agent onboarding into the Hlabi Agent OS and, where applicable, franchise pathway
- reporting and audit trails

AI assistance should remain reviewable and must not fabricate qualifications, results, regulatory status or employment outcomes.
